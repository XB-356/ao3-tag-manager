About AO3

The Archive of Our Own (AO3) is a non-profit, non-commercial fanwork archive for transformative fanworks; created by and for fans of books, music, art, games, shows, movies, real-person fiction (RPF), and other fandoms.

AO3 is built upon the principle of maximum inclusiveness of fanwork content. Fanworks that do not violate the Content Policy or some other part of the Terms of Service will not be removed from AO3, even if someone believes they are offensive or objectionable.

AO3 runs on open-source archiving software developed by the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW). Anyone is welcome to contribute by working on the unclaimed tasks in our Jira project.
